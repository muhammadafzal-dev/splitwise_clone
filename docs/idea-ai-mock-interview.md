# Idea: AI Mock Interview Platform
> Status: PARKED — needs differentiation angle before building
> Last updated: 2026-03-25

## The Problem
Generic AI interview tools already exist (Yoodli, Interviews.chat, ChatGPT).
Building another one without a clear differentiator = wasted effort.

## Why It's Still Worth Revisiting
- Multimodal AI (speech + video + text) in one app is impressive technically
- Solves a real, painful user problem
- Monetizable at $10-20/month per user

## What We Need to Decide Before Building
1. **Who is the primary user?** (fresh grads, FAANG-targeting engineers, career switchers, non-English speakers)
2. **What's the unique angle?** — The differentiation must come from specificity, not AI itself
   - Company-specific simulation (scrape job postings + culture docs)
   - Resume-to-question mapping (questions based on YOUR resume gaps)
   - Behavioral pattern tracking over time (you keep saying "um", avoiding STAR format)
   - Hiring manager perspective simulation
   - Peer + AI hybrid interviews
3. **What data layer makes it special?** — AI is the tool, data is the moat

## Parked Until
- We complete 1-2 smaller projects first
- We pick a specific user angle
- We validate there's real demand for the differentiated version

## Next Step When Resuming
Resume brainstorming session — answer the target user question first.
